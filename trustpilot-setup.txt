=============================================

CareCallAI is an all-in-one care management platform built specifically for domiciliary care agencies and care homes in Wales and England. Designed by care professionals for care professionals, CareCallAI replaces the need for multiple disconnected systems with a single, easy-to-use platform.

Our platform helps care providers stay compliant, reduce paperwork, and deliver better care with features including:

Digital care logging with GPS check-in and real-time visit tracking, giving managers full visibility of care delivery. Electronic MAR charts (eMAR) for safer, paperless medication management. Intelligent staff rota management with shift patterns, templates, and automated scheduling. Comprehensive clinical assessments including Waterlow, MUST, NEWS2, Falls Risk, Abbey Pain Scale, Barthel Index, and SALT referrals. Built-in CIW and CQC compliance management with pre-populated regulation forms and step-by-step guidance. Automated 12-weekly staff supervision tracking to meet RISCA Regulation 36 requirements. Safeguarding incident reporting with full audit trails. A family portal so loved ones can stay updated on care in real time. AI-powered care insights and risk alerts to help teams make better decisions. A mobile app for carers in the field, designed to work even with limited connectivity.

CareCallAI is proudly built in Wales and trusted by care providers across the UK. Whether you manage a small domiciliary care team or a larger residential care home, CareCallAI helps you focus on what matters most — delivering outstanding care.
