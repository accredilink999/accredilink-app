## PHASE 1: PRE-LAUNCH PREPARATION (Weeks 1-8)
### "Build the Foundation Before You Sell"

---

### Step 1: Brand & Positioning (Weeks 1-2)

**What to do:**
- Finalise the brand name (CareCall AI / AccrediLink or new name)
- Create a one-page website (landing page) with:
  - Hero headline: "Everything CIW asks for at inspection — already done, in one app"
  - 3-4 feature screenshots (compliance filing, supervision tracking, care logging)
  - "Join the waiting list" email capture form
  - Social proof placeholder (ready for testimonials)
- Set up business social media:
  - LinkedIn company page (most important for B2B care sector)
  - Facebook page (care managers use Facebook groups heavily)

**Where:** Online — use Carrd.co (£19/yr) or your Vercel deployment for landing page

**Cost:** £0-50

**Tools needed:**
- Landing page builder or custom page on your domain
- Mailchimp or ConvertKit free tier for email list
- Canva for social graphics

---

### Step 2: Get DSCR Assured Status (Weeks 1-8, ongoing)

**What to do:**
- Apply for Digital Social Care Record (DSCR) assured supplier status
- This is a government-backed accreditation that signals trust
- Many local authority contracts now REQUIRE DSCR assured status
- Application is free — involves demonstrating data standards compliance

**Where:** https://www.digitalsocialcare.co.uk/social-care-technology/digital-social-care-records/

**Why it matters:** Without this, you're locked out of LA-funded providers (a huge chunk of the market)

**Cost:** Free to apply

---

### Step 3: Recruit 3-5 Pilot Providers (Weeks 3-6)

**What to do:**
- Approach 3-5 domiciliary care providers in North Wales personally
- Offer 3 months FREE in exchange for:
  - Using the platform daily
  - Honest feedback
  - A written testimonial after 3 months
  - Permission to use their name/logo as a reference
- Ideal pilot providers:
  - Small-medium domiciliary care (10-30 service users)
  - Due a CIW inspection within 6 months (so you can capture the inspection story)
  - Frustrated with current paper/manual compliance processes

**Where:**
- Your local professional network in North Wales
- Care Forum Wales member directory
- LinkedIn connections in Welsh care sector
- CIW register (search for local providers): https://www.careinspectorate.wales/

**How to approach:**
- Personal phone call or visit (NOT cold email)
- Script: "I've built a system that does all your CIW compliance — supervisions, RI visits, Reg 80 reviews — in one app instead of paper. I'm offering 3 free months to the first 5 providers in exchange for feedback. Can I show you a 15-minute demo?"

**Cost:** £0 (your time only)

---

### Step 4: Create Demo Materials (Weeks 4-6)

**What to do:**
- Record a 3-minute product demo video (screen recording with voiceover)
  - Show: filing a Reg 73 report, clicking the ? guidance, running a supervision
  - Keep it simple — "watch how easy this is" energy
- Create a one-page PDF comparison sheet:
  - CareCall AI vs. doing it manually
  - CareCall AI vs. competitors (use the 12-feature matrix)
- Create a "CIW Inspection Readiness Checklist" as a free downloadable resource
  - Lists everything CIW inspectors look for
  - Positions CareCall AI as the solution for each item

**Where:**
- Loom.com (free) for screen recording
- Canva for PDF design
- Host on your website

**Cost:** £0

## SALES PROCESS

### For Each New Lead

```
1. Lead comes in (website, event, referral, ad)
         ↓
2. Respond within 2 hours (email/phone)
         ↓
3. Book 15-minute discovery call
   - "What system are you using now?"
   - "When is your next CIW/CQC inspection?"
   - "What's your biggest compliance headache?"
         ↓
4. Live demo (20 minutes, screen share)
   - Show their specific regulation (CIW or CQC)
   - File a sample report live
   - Show supervision tracking
   - Click the ? guidance button
         ↓
5. Offer 14-day free trial
   - Help them set up in the first session
   - Import their staff list
   - Show them the supervision due dates
         ↓
6. Follow up on Day 3, Day 7, Day 12
   - "Have you tried filing a report yet?"
   - "Can I help with anything?"
         ↓
7. Convert to paid on Day 14
   - Annual plan discount (15% off)
   - Referral code for a colleague
```
